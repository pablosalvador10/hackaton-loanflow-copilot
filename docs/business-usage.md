# LoanFlow AI Business Usage Guide

## What this system does (4 lines)
LoanFlow AI supports the lending lifecycle with AI-assisted origination and servicing experiences.
It combines conversational intake with document validation and extraction for faster applications.
It also supports customer servicing with portfolio-aware policy, fee, and eligibility guidance.
It helps teams deliver faster decisions, stronger consistency, and auditable operations.

## How to use it
1. Open the LoanFlow AI app.
2. Start an origination conversation and collect applicant details.
3. Upload supporting documents, validate quality, and extract data.
4. Review the generated application summary and approval communication.
5. For existing customers, use servicing chat for portfolio and policy questions.

## Intended business users
- Loan officers and broker support teams
- Underwriting and operations staff
- Customer servicing and call-center teams
- Lending product managers and supervisors

## What users should do next
- If extracted application details are complete and validated, move to review/decision.
- If document quality or extraction confidence is low, request re-upload or clarification.
- If servicing guidance affects borrower decisions, route to the appropriate transactional flow.
